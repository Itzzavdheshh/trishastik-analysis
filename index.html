04 — What's Holding Growth Back

4 problems that must be fixed first

💸
Severe underpricing for a 4-person team
₹30K for an Android + Web app is solo-freelancer pricing. A 4-person team has overhead, coordination cost, and QA. Industry standard for this team size is ₹75K–₹3L per project. Every project at ₹30K leaves ₹45K–₹2.7L on the table.
Critical
🔄
Zero recurring revenue — starts from zero every month
100% project-based revenue means revenue is unpredictable. No AMC contracts, no retainers, no SaaS subscriptions. Every new month requires re-selling from scratch. This is the #1 reason early startups stall.
Critical
🏷️
Freelancing platforms cap growth at commodity pricing
Fiverr/Upwork take 20% commission AND force price competition with developers from Southeast Asia. These platforms are great for first clients, terrible for building a sustainable business. They signal "cheap" to the exact premium clients Trishastik wants.
High
📡
8 service lines for 4 people — too scattered to be known for anything
The website lists Software Dev, SaaS, AI/ML, Cloud, Cybersecurity, Healthcare, Industrial, and Digital Ops. Clients don't hire generalists for premium budgets. They hire specialists. Dominating 1–2 verticals drives 3x higher referral rates and 2x pricing power.
High

05 — Growth Strategy

4 vectors to 10× revenue in 12 months

01
Highest Impact
Enter the International Market Immediately
The same React Native + Web app that earns ₹30K from an Indian client earns $1,500–$3,000 (~₹1.3L–₹2.5L) from a UK or US client. This is not a skill gap. It's an audience gap. Trishastik already has the portfolio (Pariksha Sarthi is a complete EdTech ecosystem). The work is done. Now it needs to reach the right people.
  • Rebuild LinkedIn profiles as founder-led content engines — post 1 project case study/week with tech stack, problem solved, outcome
  • Move off Fiverr, create Toptal/Contra/Arc.dev profiles — these attract $500–$3K/project clients directly
  • Target US/UK startup founders who need SaaS MVPs ($5K–$20K budget range) — they are actively looking for reliable Indian dev teams
  • One-line positioning: "We build SaaS MVPs for global founders in 8–12 weeks using React, Go, and cloud-native architecture"
Revenue Impact

02
Do This Week
Fix Pricing: Launch 3 Clear Service Packages
Stop quoting custom prices for every project. Custom quotes signal uncertainty and invite negotiation downward. Package pricing signals confidence, accelerates decisions, and justifies higher prices. This alone can double average project value within 60 days.
  • Starter — Business website with CMS + lead capture: ₹75,000
  • Business Platform — Web app + mobile app + admin dashboard: ₹2,00,000
  • SaaS MVP — Full SaaS product (auth, billing, cloud, API): ₹5,00,000+
  • Healthcare System — Appointment + patient workflow + dashboard: ₹3,00,000
  • Add AMC (Annual Maintenance Contract) to every package: 20–25% of project value/year

03
Month 3–6
Build the Recurring Revenue Engine
Currently, 0% of Trishastik's revenue is recurring. This means every month is an existential re-sell. The fix: AMC contracts after every project, a retainer option for ongoing clients, and — in 6–9 months — one own SaaS product. Even 10 AMC clients at ₹10K/month = ₹1L in guaranteed baseline revenue every month.
  • Phase 1 — AMC Contracts: Offer 6-month or 1-year maintenance contracts post every delivery. ₹6,000–₹25,000/month per client depending on project size
  • Phase 2 — Retainers: For clients with ongoing needs (weekly updates, new features), offer 20-hr/month retainers at ₹30,000/month
  • Phase 3 — Own SaaS product: Pariksha Sarthi template → license to 10 EdTech startups at ₹15,000/month each. Same codebase, recurring revenue
  • Target: 40% of revenue recurring within 9 months

04
Positioning
Pick 2 Verticals. Own Them Completely.
Trishastik already has the proof: Pariksha Sarthi = EdTech. The Healthcare service line exists. India's Healthcare IT market is $19.36B growing at 19.7% CAGR. EdTech is Kota/Patna's natural territory. Dominating two verticals means better case studies, stronger referrals, faster sales cycles, and premium pricing — because clients will pay more to hire specialists in their domain.
  • Vertical 1 — EdTech: "We built Pariksha Sarthi. We can build your EdTech platform." This is a complete portfolio piece already.
  • Vertical 2 — Healthcare Tech: Target small/mid clinics in Tier-2 India that need appointment systems, patient records, and billing. ₹1.5L–₹3L per system, AMC ₹12K/month.
  • Create 1 detailed case study for each vertical with problem → solution → tech stack → outcome → screenshots
  • Reference other services ("we also do cloud, cybersecurity") but lead with these two in all marketing

The Single Most Important Insight

Trishastik doesn't have a capability problem.
It has a positioning and pricing problem.
